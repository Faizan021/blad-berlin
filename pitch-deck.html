01 · Problem Identification

Overheating street trees & courtyards—and a gap in accessible local action

02 / 09
Ecological Challenge · Berlin & Brandenburg

1. Drought-stressed street trees & sealed inner courtyards (Hinterhöfe)

  • Ongoing summer droughts, sandy soils, and sealed asphalt put severe stress on urban street trees and residential courtyards.
  • Urban heat islands directly impact vulnerable groups—especially young Kita children and older neighbours—while reducing urban biodiversity.
Social & Educational Challenge

2. Climate worry without low-barrier, intergenerational action

  • Under-resourced Kitas, public schools & senior groups lack budget and guided formats for practical outdoor climate and biodiversity education right at their doorstep.
  • Neighbours & families want to help (as seen in civic initiatives like Gieß den Kiez), but often lack structured, community-led activities that connect tree watering, courtyard greening, and learning across generations.
  • Regional companies (SMEs & CSR teams) look for tangible, local sustainability days beyond abstract carbon offsets.
Blad · Problem Identification & Stakeholder Pain Points Faizan Ahmed & Maham Shakil
02 · Solution & Problem-Solution Fit

Three hands-on pillars connecting urban cooling with community education

03 / 09
1 · Kitas, Schools & Seniors

Intergenerational Nature & Tree-Care Workshops

Guided outdoor sessions where Kita children, school classes, families, and seniors adopt and water local street trees, identify birds and insects, and share nature knowledge across generations.

2 · Youth, Adults & Kiez

Kiez & Courtyard (Hinterhof) Citizen Science

Hands-on field sessions measuring surface and air temperatures between shaded tree beds, greened inner courtyards, and sealed asphalt—making urban heat island cooling visible and measurable.

3 · B2B Revenue Engine

Corporate Kiez Impact Days

Paid team-building & ESG action days where Berlin and Brandenburg companies plant pollinator-friendly tree beds (Baumscheiben), unseal/green shared courtyards, and cross-finance free community workshops.

Blad · Value Proposition: Practical Ecology + Intergenerational Inclusion Faizan Ahmed & Maham Shakil
03 · Target Market, Size, Structure & Competition

Defined Berlin–Brandenburg market size, B2B2C structure & differentiation

04 / 09
1 · Target Market & Size

Berlin & Brandenburg (SAM)

  • B2B Paying Market: ~10,000+ SMEs, tech firms & corporate teams in Berlin–Brandenburg seeking local ESG/CSR action days & team events.
  • Community & Education Users: ~2,900+ Kitas, 800+ schools, senior centres & residential courtyard communities (Hausgemeinschaften) needing accessible climate & nature formats.
2 · Market Structure

Dual-Sided B2B2C Impact Model

  • Primary B2B Buyers (HR / CSR / Sustainability Leads): Book turnkey half-day or full-day Corporate Kiez Impact Days.
  • Public & Housing Co-Funders: Quartiersmanagement, climate adaptation funds & housing associations (Hinterhof greening).
  • Beneficiaries: Kitas, schools & senior groups participate 100% free.
3 · Competition & Differentiation

How Blad Stands Apart

  • vs. Civic Tech (Gieß den Kiez): Digital map only; Blad provides guided on-site workshops & intergenerational community action.
  • vs. School Gardens (Acker e.V.): Requires dedicated garden plots; Blad uses existing street trees & inner courtyards right outside any doorstep.
  • vs. Generic Team Events: Blad delivers measurable local cooling & social impact.

05 · Business & Financing Model

Self-sustaining hybrid model: Corporate Impact Days fund free Kiez education

06 / 09
Revenue Stream 1 · Earned B2B

Paid Corporate Kiez Impact Days

  • Half-day or full-day guided team events for companies in Berlin & Brandenburg (15–40 employees per session).
  • Includes materials (native plants, soil, watering gear, insect hotel kits), expert facilitation, and a local neighbourhood impact summary.
  • Role in model: Primary earned-revenue engine that directly cross-subsidizes free community workshops.
Revenue Stream 2 · Public & Grants

Climate, Education & Kiez Grants

  • Targeted public funding from neighbourhood funds (Quartiersmanagement), urban climate adaptation programmes, and environmental education foundations.
  • Co-funding partnerships with housing associations (Wohnungsbaugesellschaften) for Hinterhof greening & tenant workshops.
Social Impact Delivery

100% Free for Under-Resourced Groups

  • Every paid Corporate Kiez Impact Day co-finances free workshops for Kitas, public schools, and senior groups in the same neighbourhood.
  • Pre-Registration Status: Not yet officially registered (planned legal form to be finalized in Impact Labs: gUG / gGmbH).
