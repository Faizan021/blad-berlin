## 2. The Software Side: "The Brain"

### A. Automated CSRD & ESG Verification Dashboard
Under the **EU Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD)**, over 50,000 EU companies must legally document their social (S) and environmental (E) engagement. Blatt gives corporate clients a turnkey digital verification portal:

1. **Enterprise Portal for HR & Sustainability Leads:**
   - Dedicated dashboard where companies log their annual volunteering commitments.
   - Real-time impact tracker:
     - **Employee Engagement:** Volunteer hours per department, participation rate, team wellbeing NPS score.
     - **Direct Environmental Metrics:** Gallons of water delivered, sensor-measured microclimate cooling delta (e.g. 52°C asphalt vs. 24°C canopy soil), square meters of soil mulched, pollinator nesting boxes built.
     - **Social Equity Metric:** Exact school/Kita class cross-financed with photos, thank-you letters, and student attendance.

2. **1-Click Audit-Ready CSRD Report:**
   - Generates an official, auditable PDF report formatted according to European Sustainability Reporting Standards (ESRS E4 Biodiversity & Ecosystems, ESRS S1 Own Workforce, and ESRS S3 Affected Communities).
   - Instant upload into corporate ESG filing software (Plan A, SAP Sustainability, Watershed).

## 3. The Real-World Side: "The Muscle"

### A. Turnkey On-The-Ground Delivery
- Blatt provides certified workshop facilitators, physical toolkits (deep-root watering lances, soil aeration forks, wooden assembly kits), and safety gear.
- Corporate employees get an active outdoor bonding day (3.5 hours) away from screens.

### B. The 1-for-1 Community Sponsoring Engine
- Every paid B2B workshop (€1,500 – €2,800) cross-finances **1 free workshop for 20–25 local public school or Kita children**.
- Connects corporate workers with local neighborhood youth, creating genuine intergenerational community building.
