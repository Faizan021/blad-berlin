02 · The Solution

Action-oriented environmental education across all 4 seasons

Blatt is an interactive workshop platform: We combine ecological science with learning by doing (Mitmach-Workshops):

🌸 Spring (Mar–May)

Wild Bees & Pollinators

Assembling safe, non-stinging solitary wild-bee nesting kits (Insektenhotels) and sowing native wildflower seeds.

🐝 100% Stingless Solitary Bees
☀️ Summer (Jun–Aug)

Heat Defense & Trees

Citizen science: measuring 15°C+ live cooling with infrared sensor thermometers, mapping pumps, and deep-root watering.

🌡️ -15°C Live Cooling Metric
🍂 Autumn (Sep–Nov)

Soil Health & Mulching

Aerating compacted urban soil, spreading biological woodchip mulch to insulate tree roots, and planting early bulbs.

🍂 Winter Root Frost Shield
❄️ Winter (Dec–Feb)

Winter Ecology & Birds

Building wooden bird feeding stations and winter biodiversity shelters in combined indoor-outdoor maker sessions.

03 · Business Model & Impact

Cross-Financed Business Engine

How Blatt Funds Free Community Education

  • B2B Corporate CSR Workshops: Companies pay €1,500 to €2,800 per session (12–25 employees). Includes an expert facilitator, tools, safety gear, and an official CSR impact report.
  • 1-for-1 Community Cross-Financing: Every paid corporate workshop directly sponsors 1 free workshop for a local public school or Kita class (20–25 kids).
  • Realistic Unit Economics: Material and tool kits cost ~€250–€350 per session, leaving healthy margins to cover educators and scale.
